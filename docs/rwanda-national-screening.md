## Reading this table

- **Documented facilities** are the markets, cooperatives, mills, stores, collection centres and processing works found in OpenStreetMap and Overture places, after removing look-alikes (churches, schools, hotels, clubs, shops, toilets). Where none is documented, town and village centres stand in as possible collection points and are labelled "(no facility mapped)".
- **Ranked · robust**: sites in the top 10, and how many of them stay in the top 10 in at least 80% of 300 simulations.
- **kg/person** is the district's 2024 production divided by its 2022 census population (ESTIMATED; see `data/rwanda/district_production_2024.csv`). Milk uses the national figure, as no district series is published.
- **Siting model column**: how many squares with a documented facility the shipped model puts in the district's top 10%. This is in-sample (the model was trained on all 30 districts); the out-of-sample test is in [phase4r-results](phase4r-results.md).
