# EnaGIS method and MVP evidence report

9 October 2026. **The offline product and defense materials are delivered. The full PRD's real
scientific exit remains incomplete.** No worldwide accuracy, measured energy performance or
field-confirmed top ten is claimed. The interface is a functioning evidence viewer with global
location selection; its built-in numerical analysis is the recorded temporary Canada scenario.

## Problem and intended use

A productive-use-energy planning team has limited time for visits. EnaGIS carries a ranked
investigation list, the evidence behind each number and the concrete question to check first.
Screening leads to a phone check, then a visit and a local business case. A high rank does not
establish need, operation, electrical supply, affordability or willingness to pay.

The global product direction is implemented through local world navigation and a regional
presentation contract. Search indexed countries/towns, enter any valid coordinates, or select
a point. Places without an analysis package have UNKNOWN requirement and an exportable evidence
request. A coordinate does not create production or energy evidence. New-region analysis enters
through source adapters, local configuration and separate untouched evaluation upstream.

## Pilot and registry

The frozen scientific benchmark is non-durum wheat storage and ambient-air aeration at primary
grain elevators. AB/SK development contains 261 candidates (89 Alberta, 172 Saskatchewan).
Manitoba remains the transfer holdout; no new Manitoba data were read in this release.
The 2024 AAFC location/storage source reports all-crop storage tonnes, not airflow or kW.
Coordinates are displayed as longitude/latitude EPSG:4326 and retain P2 named-place precision;
extra decimal digits are not a survey. Identity and source status are preserved.

Production uses the recorded non-durum category mapping and original statistical flags.
The pilot has 25 production origins, 16 calculable and nine unknown. Restricted reconciliation
and any personal verification data remain outside the portable application. The public
source manifest, licence audit and normalized-input audit document acquisition and joins.

## Allocation, service and supply

The displayed Phase 3 screen uses a bounded administrative equal-share scenario, with explicit
shared storage limits, known-mass conservation and unserved production. It does not use a
validated commercial road catchment. The generalized map roads and reporting regions are
display context. They do not alter assignment or establish a legal/physical route.

The separate Phase 6 engine implements directed mode/season routing, nearest-first and
max-served/min-cost allocation, joint parameter scenarios and replayable uncertainty products.
It has completed a synthetic end-to-end rehearsal and synthetic geographic checks. Its real
run still requires applicable commercial inventory/fan-duty evidence and reviewed settings.

Aeration calculation keeps storage tonnes, throughput tonnes/period, airflow m³/s, electrical
kW and cycle kWh distinct. Modeled inventory depends on residence, occupancy and throughput.
Airflow and static pressure with fan/motor efficiencies determine concurrent electrical duty;
cycle duration determines cycle electricity. Neither annual throughput nor daily kWh becomes
peak kW through an unexplained division. The display presents one hypothetical Phase 3 scenario.

Three supply states are implemented: no documented asset, documented/known compatible capacity,
and documented/unknown capacity. Unknown supply does not become zero; signed capacity-minus-
requirement remains unknown if either comparable quantity is missing. Reported storage mass
does not establish known electrical aeration capability. The built-in screen lacks consumed
installed electrical capacity observations.

## Registered siting experiment

The presence-only test is a parallel branch, with upstream label-free features and no use of
allocation, capacity, energy, gap or verification outputs as features. Protocol v1.1 was frozen,
pushed and remotely registered before evaluation. The metadata are in the registration and
recorded report. CAR spatial CV and fold-local preprocessing preserve the intended split;
unlabelled CCS are background, not confirmed absence.

The primary cohort has 155 complete CCS, 60 positive CCS and seven positive CAR blocks.
Mean CAR recall at the top 20% per block is 35.714% for the full model versus 39.762% for
production-only. The full-minus-best-baseline margin is −4.048 percentage points, with paired
95% interval [−39.286, −0.714] points. The frozen verdict is **KILL**.

A failed keep rule means **no demonstrated improvement from this proxy-based accessible-production
feature**; it is not evidence against road-catchment logic. With few CAR blocks the paired
bootstrap interval is wide and potentially unstable; report intervals and counts descriptively.
The AAFC registry is close to a census of primary elevators, so this experiment cannot test
robustness to documentation bias. Secondary/sensitivity cohorts cannot override the primary rule.

## Hindcast and comparisons

The retrospective shipping-point diagnostic compares 52 common groups across seven CAR blocks;
86 observed groups are excluded from the common comparison. CGC deliveries are kilotonnes
converted explicitly to tonnes, and each shipping-point total is counted once across its
matched elevators. Facility-level volume observations are not claimed.

Phase 3 Spearman is 0.773, with descriptive 95% interval [0.282, 0.821]. B2 is 0.657 with
[0.200, 0.815]. B0, B1 and population also appear in the recorded report. The overlapping
intervals do not establish superiority. Top-volume hits are 4/11 for Phase 3 and 8/11 for B2;
different metrics need not favor the same arm. These are retrospective ranking diagnostics,
not temporal forecasts, validated absolute volumes or energy validation. No expert shortlist
was supplied. Node-level priority and CCS/group-level test units are not interchangeable.

The two [reproducible interval figures](figures/source.json) read these recorded results;
their source sidecar preserves counts, intervals and the input hash. They do not rerun fitting.

## Uncertainty and verification

The public Phase 3 scenario has equal deterministic lower/central/upper values. The viewer
does not call those a range. It shows **range unknown** and **rank uncertainty not assessed**.
The Phase 6 implementation can produce ranges and stability tiers under approved joint inputs;
those real inputs and the real scientific exit are still absent. Inclusion frequency under
configured draws is not calibrated real-world probability or economic viability.

Phase 7 software prepares an immutable replayed ranking/sample, binds it to exact source/Git
objects and the remote tag, captures private consented observations, and produces aggregate
failure counts, intervals and missingness bounds. The saved rehearsal uses synthetic nodes,
invented responses and a local fixture tag. It is software evidence only.

Real field sample n = 0. No real `ranking-v1` exists, no real sample is frozen, no participant
commitment is supplied, and no real precision@k can be calculated. Applicable source evidence,
an approved real scientific run, the protocol choices and willing participation are prerequisites.
The prepared outreach requests have not been sent; project-completion instructions do not
create third-party consent or commitment.

## Interface, rights and reproduction

The application offers four persistent lenses, global place/coordinate search, map selection,
full evidence cards, source traces, shortlist CSV and an A4 site brief through print/Save as PDF.
No presenter launch, choreography or demonstration overlay appears in the product. A separate
presenter guide explains the defense sequence. EN/FR/RW shell settings and Simple/Expert
persist; source excerpts retain their original language. Native-speaker certification is absent.

Natural Earth world outlines/place names are pinned public-domain navigation assets. AB/SK
OSM derivatives retain ODbL attribution and a separate downloadable road file. Local Inter /
Fraunces are SIL OFL 1.1 and MapLibre is BSD-3-Clause. No external services, tracking, accounts,
closed keys or runtime calls are required. Optional source/phone-map links need connectivity;
device geolocation needs browser permission and may be unavailable offline.

After Python 3.12 / the pinned uv environment is installed, one offline release command is:

```sh
uv run --offline --locked --cache-dir .uv-cache python -m scripts.release_mvp --check
```

This checks code, rebuilds the application, generates figures and writes a deterministic
`outputs/mvp-release/EnaGIS-MVP.zip` plus a hash audit. `app/index.html` opens directly.
The separate browser acceptance script uses supplied Playwright and Edge; it is not a product
runtime dependency. A mechanical portable rebuild is checked; independent human clean-machine
review and timed live defense rehearsals are not claimed.
