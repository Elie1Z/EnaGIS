## Source observations and limits

1. The [Rwanda geodata Coffee Washing Stations layer](https://geodata.rw/server/rest/services/basemap/Coffee_Washing_Stations/FeatureServer/0) is a point layer credited to NAEB, MINICOM and RLA. On 7 October 2026, its public `query?where=1%3D1&returnCountOnly=true&f=pjson` returned **207**; all 207 query records had point geometry. District-grouped counts sum to **84** for Rutsiro 15, Rusizi 19, Karongi 11, Rubavu 3, Nyabihu 1, Nyamasheke 31 and Ngororero 4. All 84 Western records have a sector value; eight national records have a blank district and seven have a blank sector. The layer's survey labels span **2006–2011**; this is a historical location lead, not proof of 2026 operation. Its published fields contain status and survey date but no capacity or throughput. Its spatial reference is a custom ITRF 2005 projection: transform only after verifying the WKT, never assume EPSG:4326. Copyright text is not a redistribution licence.
2. The [NAEB 2023/24 annual report](https://www.naeb.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/NAEB/Publications/Reports/Annual_reports/NAEB_Annual_report_2023-2024_.pdf) reports **313 active CWS** and **56% aggregate capacity utilisation** in 2024. This conflicts with the older point-layer count as a complete current registry; neither figure supplies 30 station-level volumes.
3. The [NAEB 2018/19 annual report](https://www.naeb.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/NAEB/Publications/Reports/Annual_reports/ANNUAL_REPORT_2018-2019.pdf) gives provincial CWS counts, capacity and cherries processed, including **128 Western Province stations** in 2019. These are aggregate historical figures, not per-station observations or present-day status.
4. The [NAEB Coffee Trees per Sector layer](https://gis.naeb.gov.rw/server/rest/services/Hosted/Coffee_trees_per_Sector/FeatureServer/info/iteminfo) identifies a 2015 census proxy. The layer fields include `coffee_trees_2015`; tree count is not measured tonnes of cherries. Its item metadata has no licence text.
5. [NAEB's horticulture project page](https://www.naeb.gov.rw/spiu-projects/project-funded-by-enabel) targets 40 cooperatives/SMEs in 13 districts. The [2018/19 report](https://www.naeb.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/NAEB/Publications/Reports/Annual_reports/ANNUAL_REPORT_2018-2019.pdf) describes one central packhouse and its cold rooms. These sources do not establish a 60-site cold-chain registry.
6. The [MINAGRI 2024/25 annual report](https://www.minagri.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minagri/Publications/Annual_Reports/Annual_report_2024-2025_Final.pdf) counts maize shelters, rice drying grounds, warehouses and grain dryers nationally. The [RAB 2023/24 annual report](https://www.rab.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RAB/Publications/Reports/RAB_Annual_2023-24.pdf) lists some private grain dryers and capacities. These are useful leads, but mixing drying grounds, stores and mills would violate the processing/service gate.

## Audit tasks before signing decision 0001

1. Export a dated station snapshot; deduplicate and count records with usable sector/coordinates and current operating evidence, nationally and in the proposed pilot.
2. Seek station-level cherry receipts/throughput or defined capacities for at least 30 distinct stations; otherwise name the district-total hindcast fallback and its limits.
3. Name the person/office who can arrange a 12–20 site verification sample, and record the contact method and consent protocol privately.
4. Obtain a processing-energy source/range and a product-value/tariff basis for the value-at-stake check.
5. Confirm licence terms before mirroring point records, tree counts or derived geometry publicly.
