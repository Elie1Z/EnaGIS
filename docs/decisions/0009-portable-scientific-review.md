## Scientific boundary

Human approval is required by AGENTS.md and the PRD; outsourcing to an external consultant is
not an additional mandatory gate. Approval must concern actual values and their applicability.
Generic guidance cannot identify the missing commercial operating conditions at the candidates.
The current kernel uses global coefficients per draw; a justified site-class or nonlinear fan
model needs a separately specified, implemented and tested contract before real execution.

This continuation acquires no empirical datasets, changes no Phase 4 method/result, consumes
no Manitoba outcomes, and creates no real Phase 6 ranking or Phase 7 freeze. Current synthetic
artifacts have new code hashes; historical run evidence is retained with its source version.
Software portability is demonstrated by invented geometry, not worldwide prediction accuracy.

Evidence and verification are recorded in [the desk review](../phase6-desk-review.md) and its
[machine audit](../audits/phase6-desk-review.json). Phase 6 remains open until sourced inputs,
human decisions, a real replay and the prospectively specified comparison satisfy its exit.
