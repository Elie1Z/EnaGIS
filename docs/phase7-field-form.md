# Phase 7 private verification form — proposed, not yet authorized for field use

Use the exact consent and decision criteria from the approved frozen protocol. This draft
supports review; it is not permission to contact anyone. No respondent or contact details
belong in this public document. Keep completed forms, contact lists and supporting evidence
under `data/validation_private/` with access limited to the team authorized by the protocol.

## Before contact

Record privately the freeze ID, manifest hash, sampled node ID, assessor reference, local time
with timezone, method, and the sampled node's single verification question. Confirm the node is
in the frozen sample and the seal has passed verification. Do not substitute an easier contact
or replace an unreachable sampled node. Use the approved identity/location matching procedure.

The blank JSON capture is created by `init-records` only after sealing. It initially states
`not_contacted`, `not_requested` consent and null factual answers for every sampled node.

## Consent and contact status

Read the frozen consent script. Record granted or declined consent. If the person declines,
stop the questionnaire; record only the permitted contact/consent status. If unreachable, keep
every factual answer unknown. A missing answer is never a negative or positive observation.
Keep any allowed attempt history private and maintain one final adjudicated row per sampled node.

## Questions for a consenting participant

1. Does this specific aggregation/storage location exist at the recorded place? Establish the
   match; distinguish missing location evidence, temporary closure and a genuinely absent node.
2. What wheat-storage/aeration activity occurs here, during which season and reporting period?
   Separate current conditions from the retrospective production and registry period.
3. Which aeration assets operate here? What compatible airflow/electrical capacity is documented,
   and what evidence supports the simultaneous duty? Storage tonnes do not establish fan kW.
4. What inventory, use and operating evidence bears on the approved minimum service requirement?
   Record uncertainty and refer numerical claims to their source. Do not invent an unknown rating.
5. Does the activity matter in the protocol's target decision season? Seasonal operation alone
   does not establish irrelevance; apply the approved criterion and preserve unknown answers.
6. Ask the specific question stored for this node in the frozen sample. Keep its supporting
   material private and assign an opaque evidence reference for the adjudicated record.

## Adjudication and submission

Apply the protocol's criteria to `node_exists`, `undocumented_supply`,
`requirement_negligible` and `seasonal_irrelevance`; each can remain null. Retain a private
evidence reference and assessor reference. A newly documented asset is not automatically a
false opportunity: use the reviewed capacity/service comparison and criterion.

The primary outcome uses the frozen precedence: absent node, undocumented supply, negligible
requirement, seasonal irrelevance, then confirmed. An earlier unknown prevents resolution to a
later category. Record all observed flags even when only one primary failure is assigned.
`confirmed` means a screening candidate merits further technical investigation under the
approved definition. It is not a feasibility, investment or bankability conclusion.

Validate before analysis. Correct observation errors in a new private records version with
an audit trail; never edit the frozen ranking, sample, protocol or tag to improve the result.
Publish only the aggregate report, its denominators and limits. A new post-verification method
or ranking requires a separately identified analysis and must disclose the outcomes it used.
