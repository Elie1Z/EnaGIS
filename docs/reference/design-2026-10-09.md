MASTER DESIGN BRIEF: EnaGIS interface (supersedes any earlier visual direction)

ROLE
You are the lead product designer and front-end engineer for EnaGIS, a universal screening
system: it shows where to investigate first for productive-use energy in agricultural value
chains, and how sure we are. The scientific pipeline and its outputs are almost finished. Your
job is the interface layer only. You consume existing outputs and never change science.

NORTH STAR
"Map first, lenses second, one honest story always." A real, navigable map is the constant. Users
pick a LENS by what they want to do. Every screen answers four questions in this order:
WHAT is it, WHY here, HOW SURE, WHAT NEXT. Anyone should understand the first screen in under
10 seconds without training.

ART DIRECTION: "Survey sheet, modernised"
Light only. Warm paper basemap, hairline ink, one ember accent. Calm, precise, confident. It
should feel like a field surveyor's sheet made modern, never like a neon dashboard.
- Tokens (CSS variables):
  --paper #F7F5F0 (app bg) · --land #EFEBE2 · --water #DCE6EA · --card #FFFFFF
  --ink #1B2430 · --ink-2 #4A5568 · --ink-3 #6B7686 · --hair #E4DFD5
  --ember #D9480F (need, primary action) · --teal #0F766E (verified, confirmed, focus ring)
  --amber #B7791F (estimated) · --slate #5B6B7F (inferred) · --violet #6D5BD0 (predicted)
  Evidence label chips use pattern plus text plus color; UNKNOWN is a gray hatch, never zero.
- Type (self-host variable fonts, OFL, no CDN): Fraunces for titles, big numbers and the "Check
  first" line (set in italic, like a field note); Inter for all UI with tabular numerals.
  Scale: 28/32 page title · 20/24 card title · 14/20 body · 12/16 meta · 11 uppercase labels,
  +0.08em tracking. Minimum 14px for body text. No other fonts.
- Shape and depth: cards radius 12; pills 999; hairline borders; shadows only
  0 1px 2px rgba(20,25,35,.06) and 0 8px 24px rgba(20,25,35,.08). 4px spacing grid.
  Touch targets at least 44px. AA contrast minimum everywhere. Visible 2px teal focus ring.
- Icons: simple 1.5px line icons, one consistent set. No emoji. No stock illustrations.

VISUAL GRAMMAR (identical in every lens; show it once in a "How to read this map" popover)
1. SIZE of marker = estimated energy requirement (square-root scale).
2. RING STYLE = confidence: solid ring = sharp/confident; dashed = soft; feathered/double
   outline = blurry. Also shown in words (Sharp / Soft / Blurry).
3. HAZE (fine diagonal hatch over the map) = thin map data, so "not yet known", not "absent".
Rank number sits inside the marker. Verified sites get a teal check. Never use blobs or heatmaps
that imply precision.

MAP
Real map with pan, zoom, rotate-lock, search and geolocate. MapLibre GL JS with a custom light,
desaturated, low-contrast open basemap (OSM-derived, vendored for offline). Hide POI clutter.
OSM attribution always visible. Catchments: soft fill plus hairline ink stroke, only on selection.
No third-party map keys. Keep selection and camera when switching lens.

LENSES (segmented control, plain-language labels; four only)
1. "Where first" (default): numbered Top-10 markers, others dimmed; ranked list; node card.
   First load shows the Top-10 immediately; no splash screen; one line: "Start here: the 10
   places to look first."
2. "How sure": tiers Robust / Contested / Verify first with range bars; evidence labels; the
   comparison with simpler baselines ("what changed and why"); validation results with sample
   sizes and intervals; the limitations note.
3. "How to get there": roads emphasised; dry/rainy toggle plus fine slider; access status;
   best visit window; pre-visit checklist; copy coordinates; open in phone maps. Animated flow
   lines only here, only on the selected site, and off under reduced motion.
4. "How it works": a 5-step guided story (harvest, collection points, catchments, requirement,
   what we do not know, how we verify) that drives the camera and layers.
Layer chips (small, optional): Roads · Catchment · Energy context (label "low confidence") · Thin-data haze.

ONE DETAIL SWITCH FOR DIFFERENT AUDIENCES
"Simple / Expert". Simple: words only (Sharp/Soft/Blurry, "about 120 to 210 kWh/day"). Expert:
intervals, evidence labels, ranks versus baselines, sources and dates. Persist the choice.

LAYOUT
Desktop: slim left rail (lens switcher), left drawer (ranked list and filters), full-bleed map,
right detail sheet for the selected site, top bar (search, season, Simple/Expert, language
EN/FR/RW, help). Bottom-left: scale plus "How to read this map". Mobile: map full screen, bottom
tab bar for lenses, draggable bottom sheet with three snap heights; the map stays visible.

NODE CARD (the signature component)
Title and rank. Then three blocks: "Why it is here" (one sentence, with numbers), "How sure"
(confidence word plus range), "Check first" (one concrete question, Fraunces italic). Footer:
evidence chips (Need: ESTIMATED · Capacity: UNKNOWN or OBSERVED · Location: OBSERVED) with
source and date on tap. Actions: copy coordinates, download one-page site brief (A4, print-ready).

WRITING RULES
Plain verbs and short sentences. Say "Check first", not "Verify-first tier". Say "unserved
requirement", not "energy gap". Every number carries a unit and, if estimated, a range, rounded
to two significant digits. Never write probability language such as "78% chance". Never show
UNKNOWN as zero or blank. Keep a visible line: "Screening tool, not a feasibility study" linking to
the limitations page.

ACCESSIBILITY AND ROBUSTNESS
Colour is never the only encoding. Full keyboard operation of list, markers and lenses. The ranked
list is the accessible equivalent of the map. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Text resizes to 200%.
Meaningful empty, loading and error states (for example "No data for this area yet").

TECHNICAL RULES
Static site, offline-capable, no tracking, no network at runtime. Fonts, map library and tiles
vendored. Data via the existing shortlist and GeoJSON outputs. Create a thin view-model adapter in
the front end for any missing fields; never invent data. If data is synthetic, show a "Synthetic
data" ribbon. Do not edit pipeline code, protocol or config files, registration or hashed files,
or anything under outputs except new front-end build artefacts.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No dark mode. No 3D globe. No neon or decorative gradients. No heatmap blobs. No auto-playing loops
outside the Field lens flow. No carousels. No modal pop-ups in core flows. No more than four
lenses. No false precision. No unlabeled estimates.
