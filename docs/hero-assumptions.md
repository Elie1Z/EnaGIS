Generated from the shipped configuration; reporting does not change its values. Scientific parameters remain unapproved screening assumptions. ASSUMED qualifies unsupported choices; original engine evidence labels are preserved in JSON. No field verification (n = 0).

## Calculation

Intake tonnes/day = effective catchment people × district tonnes/person/year × capture share × site-type multiplier × peak-month fraction ÷ 30. Stored tonnes = intake × storage days. Electricity kWh/day = (intake × cp × temperature difference ÷ 3.6 + stored × holding load) ÷ COP. Storage mass, intake flow, thermal energy and electrical energy remain distinct.

## Sensitivity

The hero tornado reports an order-averaged linear allocation of variance using the same 300 draws, including an explicit residual. It does not partition the 5th–95th percentile interval and is not causal attribution. Running hours affects kW, not kWh, and is excluded from this tornado. Rain factors are fixed scenarios, not sampled inputs. Rank departures in joint draws cannot be attributed to a single assumption. The separate one-at-a-time rank check uses shipped low/high ranges; if no assumption pushes a top-3 site below rank 10, that negative finding is reported.
