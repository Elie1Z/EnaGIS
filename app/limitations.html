EVIDENCE AND SCOPE

Screening tool, not a feasibility study

EnaGIS helps teams decide where to investigate first for productive uses of energy in agricultural value chains. Every assessment is tied to a source package, a period and a declared service.

A world map, local evidence

You can search indexed places, enter latitude, longitude or select a point anywhere. The offline place index is incomplete. Natural Earth's world outlines are coarse navigation context; small islands, disputed boundaries and streets may be omitted or generalized. These shapes do not define analysis coverage.

The built-in evidence covers Alberta and Saskatchewan, non-durum wheat storage and ambient-air aeration, in a retrospective 2024/25 context. Manitoba is an untouched scientific transfer region. Worldwide navigation does not establish worldwide model accuracy. New regions need source adapters, local parameters and untouched evaluation.

What the current numbers support

The shortlist presents the recorded Phase 3 temporary engineering scenario. Production assignment, inventory and fan duty are hypothetical. It is ordered by scenario fan kW and stable node identifiers; ties do not establish a difference in urgency. Storage mass in tonnes is different from electrical capacity in kW. Electrical supply is undocumented in the consumed registry.

The deterministic scenario has no assessed rank uncertainty or defensible energy interval. Identical endpoints are not an uncertainty interval. Robust / Contested tiers, seasonal route results, travel-time access, tariffs, grid access and a best visit window are UNKNOWN here. Missing documentation is not evidence of absence.

The registered test

The recorded Phase 4 experiment used 155 complete CCS, 60 positive CCS and seven positive CAR blocks. The full model's mean CAR recall at the top 20% was 35.7%; production-only was 39.8%. Full minus best baseline was −4.05 percentage points, with a paired 95% margin interval of −39.29 to −0.71 points. The verdict is KILL.

A failed keep rule means no demonstrated improvement from this proxy-based accessible-production feature; it is not evidence against road-catchment logic. With few CAR blocks the paired bootstrap interval is wide and potentially unstable; report intervals and counts descriptively.

The AAFC registry is close to a census of primary elevators, so this experiment cannot test robustness to documentation bias. The shipping-point hindcast is a separate retrospective diagnostic; it does not validate energy, absolute volumes or a future forecast. See the recorded report and registered protocol.

Verification

No real verification observations have been collected (n = 0). The Phase 7 freeze/capture/analysis software has passed a synthetic rehearsal. There is no real ranking-v1 freeze or approved verification sample. Synthetic test outcomes are excluded from this application. Applicable operating evidence, scientific sign-off and a willing participant remain required for the real scientific exit.
