# Code of conduct

EnaGIS adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive; welcome people of every background, country, language
and level of experience; critique evidence and code, not people; no harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks or publishing others' private information. Field partners and data holders
deserve the same respect, as does their confidentiality.

**Reporting.** Contact the maintainer, Elie Mugisha, through the e-mail on their GitHub profile
(https://github.com/Elie1Z), or open a private security advisory on the repository. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove contributions or contributors that violate this
code, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
