# Data

| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| [`rwanda/`](rwanda/README.md) | Inputs for EnaGIS Live: district production, the national siting table, and the frozen Nyabihu case with its verification kit (`rwanda/hero/`). |
| `manual/` | Small hand-made tables with provenance: facility identities, the Phase 4 registration record, and the Phase 6 site-evidence template. Hash-registered; do not edit. |
| `raw/`, `interim/`, `processed/` | Created locally by `make acquire` and `make ingest` for the Canadian pilot. Not committed (see `.gitignore`); pinned by SHA-256 in [docs/data/manifest.json](../docs/data/manifest.json). |
